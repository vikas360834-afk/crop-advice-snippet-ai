## Provenance limits

The JSONL records do not contain verified publication or retrieval dates. Those dates are intentionally not inferred. A URL and source label establish the declared attribution for a record; they do not independently establish that the summary is complete, current, or suitable for every region. Review the linked source and local extension guidance before acting on agricultural advice.
